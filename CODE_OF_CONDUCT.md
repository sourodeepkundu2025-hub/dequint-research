# Code of Conduct

Discuss scientific methods and evidence respectfully. Critique a claim, model,
implementation or result rather than the person reporting it. Different or
negative findings are welcome when their methods and limitations are explicit.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and disclosure of another
person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post private reviewer
correspondence, unpublished third-party work, credentials or personal records.

Maintainers may ask for clarification, edit or remove harmful material, lock a
discussion or restrict participation. Raise a concern privately with the repository
maintainer through their GitHub profile; do not include sensitive details in a
public issue. This policy applies to repository discussions, issues and reviews.
